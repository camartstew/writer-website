Cameron Stewart

Cameron Stewart is a writer and educator based in Brooklyn. His work has appeared in Tin House and EPOCH Magazine, among others, and he has received fellowships from the Sewanee Writers' Conference and Columbia University's School of the Arts.

As an editor, Cameron has over a decade of experience providing customized writing support. Through personalized edits and private tutoring, he has helped thousands of clients find their voices as writers. He is currently accepting new projects for copy editing, personal statement coaching, and freelance writing assignments.

Services

Personal Statement Coaching

One-on-one guidance for crafting compelling personal statements. From college applications to fellowship essays, I help you tell your story in a way that stands out to admissions committees and selection panels.

Copy Editing

Comprehensive editing for clarity, flow, and impact. I work with manuscripts, articles, and digital content to ensure your writing resonates with readers while maintaining your unique voice.

Freelance Writing

Custom content creation for publications, websites, and marketing materials. I specialize in narrative journalism, personal essays, and feature writing across diverse topics and audiences.
