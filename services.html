Services

Everything here stays in your voice. The reference is visual inspiration only—no language is copied. Below is a clear outline of what I do and how we’ll work together.

Overview

Personal Statement Coaching

One-on-one guidance for crafting compelling personal statements. From college applications to fellowship essays, I help you tell your story in a way that stands out to admissions committees and selection panels.

Copy Editing

Comprehensive editing for clarity, flow, and impact. I work with manuscripts, articles, and digital content to ensure your writing resonates with readers while maintaining your unique voice.

Freelance Writing

Custom content creation for publications, websites, and marketing materials. I specialize in narrative journalism, personal essays, and feature writing across diverse topics and audiences.

Options

Personal Statement Coaching — Options

Sprint. One focused round to sharpen a single statement. You’ll get margin notes, a short revision plan, and an optional 30–45 minute session.

Comprehensive. Two rounds across one or more statements, with a strategy memo that clarifies story, structure, and tone.

Mentorship. Ongoing support over a season with scheduled check‑ins and iterative feedback.

Copy Editing — Options

Line Edit. Clarity, rhythm, and style at the sentence level while preserving your voice.

Line + Light Developmental. Sentence‑level polish plus notes on structure, pacing, and emphasis.

Manuscript Review. A deeper read culminating in a concise editorial letter and a prioritized roadmap.

Freelance Writing — Options

Commissioned Features & Essays. Reporting‑driven features and personal essays.

Organizations & Sites. Clear, human copy for pages, newsletters, and announcements.

Ongoing Support. A cadence for regular content with light editorial planning.
